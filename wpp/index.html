What's Poppin Popcorn

A brand refresh, a more considered digital storefront, and practical systems for the customer journey.

Role
Brand, Digital and Automation Consultant
Focus
Brand, website and customer operations
Duration
2025 to 2026

01 The work

A familiar business, entering a new phase.

After Eagle Heights acquired What's Poppin Popcorn, I led a refresh of the brand and digital presence. The aim was to bring the business into its next chapter while keeping the local character and customer familiarity that were already there.

That work reached beyond the website. The refreshed identity carried through social media, menus, in-store materials, gift cards, seasonal campaigns and online ordering. I also supported the consolidation of digital and operational systems, including website management, business email and coordination with ordering and delivery providers.

Brand and communication

Refined the visual language and messaging, then applied them across customer touchpoints.

Website and ordering

Updated the digital storefront and connected customer paths to the available ordering channels.

Digital operations

Helped bring the tools behind the business into a more manageable setup.

02 Customer enquiries

The website form, connected to an internal intake.

Customers use the enquiry form on the website to ask about an event and request a quote. A script passes each submission into Google Forms, creating a consistent internal record that can start the follow-up process. Customers do not see or fill in the Google Form.

03 One workflow

A more organised way to handle enquiries.

As one part of the wider work, I built an enquiry workflow in Activepieces. It receives each submission, validates and organises the details, uses AI to support lead qualification and draft a response, then saves the customer record and prepares an email for review.

The automation takes care of the repetitive preparation. A person reviews the record and response before following up with the customer.

04 What changed

A smoother path from enquiry to response.

Customers can move from interest to a quote request through a clear form, while the team receives the details in a consistent format. The workflow organises each enquiry, prepares a customer record and drafts a reply for review, reducing repetitive handling and making follow-up easier.

Alongside a more consistent brand across the website, ordering channels and everyday materials, these changes make the business easier to engage with and simpler to run. The enquiry workflow is one part of a broader set of brand, digital and operations work, which also included advice on measurement and opportunities in gifting, events and corporate orders.
