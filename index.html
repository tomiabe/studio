The person behind the studio

I'm Tomi Abe, a creative technologist with a background in computer science and postgraduate study in creative technology. The studio began in 2013 and has evolved alongside my work in consulting, design leadership, software development, research and creative experimentation.

I lead the practice personally and collaborate with specialists when a project calls for it. Beyond the studio, I co-founded Susbridge and co-publish Susinsight. Writing, teaching and mentoring are also part of my practice.
