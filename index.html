Selected work

Editorial platform

Susinsight

A digital home for sustainability reporting across Africa.

Visit Susinsight
Role
Creative and platform lead
Period
2023 to present
Focus
Brand, product, editorial systems

01 / The challenge

Make complex reporting easier to explore.

Susinsight covers the people, policies, and ideas shaping sustainability across Africa. Its growing editorial work needed a clear identity and a platform that could support stories, data, and new ways of finding information.

02 / The work

One system across brand and platform.

The studio shaped the visual identity and creative direction, then moved the publication from a traditional WordPress setup to a Next.js platform with Markdown content managed in Pages CMS. A shared design system brings consistency to stories, data features, newsletters, and the wider digital presence.

03 / For readers and editors

Useful beyond the homepage.

A glossary, data-led stories, events, audio, and Ask Susinsight give readers more ways into the reporting. Behind the scenes, an extensible taxonomy and an editorial workflow help the team publish and maintain it. The collaboration continues as the platform evolves.
