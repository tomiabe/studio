Eze A visual identity for value exchange

I initiated and led a new identity system for a growing B2B electronics marketplace, creating a clearer expression of trust, exchange, and global scale across product and brand.

Period
2020 to 2021
Role
Brand & Product Design Lead
Scope
Identity strategy, design system, and rollout

The identity needed to catch up with the business

As Eze introduced new marketplace features, the existing visual language could not express the confidence, flexibility, or connected experience the business needed. I proposed a practical system that could unite product, marketing, sales, and partner touchpoints.

Make exchange visible

Buying and selling needed one clear, recognisable signal that could hold the marketplace together.

Work at every size

The identity needed to remain clear from an app tile to a marketplace interface or partner communication.

Give teams shared rules

Reusable standards would help a growing team create with confidence across every business surface.

From a wordmark to a system built for exchange

The new direction moved beyond a simple name and gave Eze an ownable mark. The interlocking e and z make the two-sided movement of value visible in one compact, adaptable symbol.

An e and z in exchange

The mark turns the name and the core marketplace interaction into one responsive form. It gives Eze a distinctive asset that works independently when the full wordmark is not needed.

Meaning
Value exchange
Form

Function
Small to large formats

A visual language built for clarity

Inter brought a direct, legible voice to the identity. Blue established recognition and action, navy brought confidence to dense information, and linen made customer-facing surfaces feel open and approachable.

A working identity, ready to travel

The system was designed to make Eze recognisable wherever a buyer, seller, or partner encountered it. These application frames will be replaced with the original rollout work.

From brand to product

I worked across product, engineering, sales, and marketing as the system moved into the website, trading platform, and customer communication. This helped the brand and product grow from one shared language.

One language across the business

What began as a self-directed initiative became the foundation for a more coherent marketplace. It gave the design team and its partners a practical language for making high-value electronics trade easier to recognise and trust.
