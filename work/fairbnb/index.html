Fairbnb

Bringing fair stays, local experiences, and community benefit into one trip people can shape for themselves.

Period
2022 to 2023
Role
Brand and Product Design Consultant
Focus
Guest journey, platform integration, interaction design, design system

How can a trip feel whole when its best reasons to exist live in separate places?

Fairbnb had more than a place to stay. It had a cooperative model, locally rooted experiences, and community projects that could benefit from each booking. The work was to make those ideas legible as one guest decision, without forcing a traveller through separate products.

That meant treating a destination as the organising principle. A guest could arrive with one intention, then choose how much of the trip to build around it.

A city is more useful than a product category.

Travellers do not begin with a mental split between accommodation and activities. They begin with a place they want to understand. The entry experience therefore puts the destination, dates, and people travelling at the centre.

A guest should build a trip, not reconcile separate bookings.

The Trip Builder is the connective tissue of the platform. It lets a stay and an experience relate to the same dates, the same group, and the same destination. It also gives the selected community project a meaningful place beside the practical details of booking.

  1. ChooseAdd a stay or an experience from a shared search context.
  2. ComposeKeep the details of both choices together as the trip takes shape.
  3. CommitReview the booking and its local contribution in one transaction.
  4. ReturnManage the confirmed trip, messages, and project context in one place.

The work gave Fairbnb a coherent product direction: a journey that moves from a destination, to discovery, to a composed trip, to an ongoing connection with the people and place behind it.

It also established a visual and interaction language for the entire guest lifecycle, rooted in Fairbnb’s existing identity and designed as a functioning vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript prototype.
